What to do while you arrange help

Keep people and pets away from water that may create a slipping or electrical hazard. If it’s safe, move belongings out of the affected area, but don’t delay your call to clean up. Avoid using the affected fixture, and don’t remove covers, disconnect pipes, or pour products into a drain while you’re unsure what caused the problem.

Have these details ready:
- The exact address where help is needed
- The room or fixture involved
- Whether water is still flowing or the condition has stopped
- Any shutoff you used, if you could reach it safely
- A clear way for the plumber to contact you

Ask what you should leave alone until the problem is checked. If you’re weighing whether a situation can wait, explain the impact on the household and let the plumber advise you on the next step.
